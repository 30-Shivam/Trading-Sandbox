=== STRATEGIC PIVOT (new top priority, supersedes pure parameter-tuning) ===

Context: after three independent rigor upgrades this session (entry-fill timing realism, tune-vs-ticker-holdout, v3-vs-ticker-holdout), the apparent edge kept collapsing toward zero from every direction rather than holding steady -- that pattern (each correction eating into the edge, all converging on the same "thin, near-zero-to-negative out-of-sample" conclusion) is the signature of a backtest edge that was substantially a measurement artifact, not genuine predictive signal being obscured by noise. Reinforcing evidence: v3's rsi_oversold_threshold has been pushed by every Optuna search (original tuning, candidate v5, v7, v9) UP toward 52ish -- barely below neutral (50), not a real "oversold" reading -- and v3 fires constantly (2021 trades on just 27 tickers in one test) at only a 20.88% win rate, staying near-breakeven only because of its asymmetric stop/target (0.5/1.86 ATR), a payoff shape that can look similar with zero real timing skill behind it. Conclusion, agreed with user: pure RSI-oversold mean-reversion is likely near its ceiling -- further parameter search within this same signal is unlikely to meaningfully improve things. Priority shifts to validating whether the CURRENT signal carries any real information at all, then (depending on that answer) adding genuinely different/complementary signal sources rather than continuing to retune the same one.

    The technical build is straightforward -- a "chart agent" (rendered price/volume chart image fed to a vision-capable model) and a "news agent" (headlines/fundamentals fed to a text model), each scoring a ticker independently, combined into an AI_Score field displayed alongside Trade_Score (NOT blended into it).
